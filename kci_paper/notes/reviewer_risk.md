# Reviewer Risk and Mitigation Strategies

This document lists potential avenues of reviewer attacks (criticism) and provides defensive framing strategies to defend the paper during peer review.

| Risk / Attack Point | Severity | Reviewer Concern | Mitigation Strategy |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Incremental Novelty (Simple combination of GNN, MAPPO, KD)** | **High** | "This is just standard CTDE using established GNN encoders and KD policy mimicry." | Emphasize the hybrid design: we do not rely purely on machine learning. We combine geographic routing priors (Greedy progress) with policy-residuals. Focus on the **Predictive Risk Switch (PRS)** as an adaptive gateway that ensures zero online communication footprint and selectively runs ML inferences only during anomalies. |
| **Simplified Simulation (Python vs. ns-3/OPNET)** | **High** | "A custom Python simulator abstracts wireless physical layer dynamics (multipath, MAC collisions, etc.)." | Acknowledge this limitation honestly. Position the Python Lite-GLOBE environment as a reproducible validation platform for routing logic, analogous to Gym environments in robotics. Highlight that the inputs are 1-hop local beacons which are easily mapped to real radio metrics. State ns-3 cross-validation as planned future work. |
| **Unrealistic Teacher (Oracle Assumption)** | **Medium** | "The global graph information used by the teacher is unrealistic and too strong." | Frame the teacher strictly as a **training-time privilege** (Centralized Training, Decentralized Execution). Emphasize that the teacher is **never deployed online** on real UAVs. Its sole purpose is to compress its global topological awareness into localized MLP weights. |
| **Statistical Validity (Multi-seed & Error Bars)** | **Medium** | "Are the improvements statistically significant or just random seed fluctuations?" | Leverage the Phase 12 full runs: 14 scenarios × 5 seeds × 200 episodes = 84,000 runs. Present mean, standard deviation, and paired scenario t-tests to prove statistical significance. Clear error bars are presented in the results section. |
| **Overhead Quantification (Input-bytes vs. Control-bytes)** | **Medium** | "Input byte footprint is a proxy, not actual network control byte overhead." | Clearly explain that input-bytes measure the local inference footprint. Differentiate between data planes (strictly localized) and control planes. Present qualitative arguments on why our strictly decentralized execution eliminates multi-hop consensus overhead, and mark per-packet control bytes as a future verification target. |
| **Phase 13 P+ Evidence Gap** | **Medium** | "The paper proposes P+ features, but reports statistical figures of Phase 12." | Position the Phase 12 configuration (Risk-Switch Lite-GLOBE-P) as the primary verified baseline. Introduce Phase 13 P+ as a proposed extension specifically targeting severe link-loss settings, leaving its full-scale seed evaluation as an explicit, open TODO in the discussion section. |
