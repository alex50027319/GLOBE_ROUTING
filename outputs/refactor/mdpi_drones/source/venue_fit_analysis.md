# Venue fit analysis

Primary target: **MDPI Drones**, Article.

Reasons:

- The subject is decentralized routing and resilience for UAV swarms/FANETs.
- A 2026 Drones paper on intelligent UAV cluster maintenance and routing provides direct topical precedent.
- The manuscript can follow the journal's expected sequence: Introduction; Materials and Methods; Results; Discussion; Conclusions; declarations and data availability.
- The project already has an official MDPI class bundle. Its `mdpi.cls` header is dated 13 March 2026 and its SHA-256 is recorded in the evidence inventory.

Risks:

- The routing results are simulation-only and the A100 is not onboard UAV hardware.
- Five seeds limit exact nonparametric significance.
- Adapted baselines must not be represented as full reproductions of their source papers.
- The authors must re-download/check the official template and current author instructions immediately before submission.

Alternative positioning: IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology or IEEE Internet of Things Journal would fit the topic but would likely demand stronger target-device/testbed evidence, larger-scale validation, and their own current template.
