Dear Editors,

Please consider our manuscript, ``Fused Exact SwitchGLOBE: Exact-Semantics Global-to-Local Policy Distillation with Risk-Switched Predictive Recovery for Decentralized UAV Routing,'' as an Article in \emph{Drones}.

The manuscript addresses a practical tension in reinforcement-learning-based FANET routing: global graph information can improve learning, but decentralized relays require local observations and batch-one decisions; meanwhile, reducing inference cost through approximation can degrade delivery reliability. We contribute a strict global-training/local-execution architecture, an interpretable risk switch between normal and predictive local policies, and an exact-semantics fused execution path that removes redundant branch evaluations without changing the routing policy.

The evaluation uses eight common-simulator methods, five training seeds, 14 held-out or stress scenarios, and 200 episodes per seed--scenario cell. SwitchGLOBE Exact attains 0.9053 connected-pair PDR and 0.8376 deadline delivery. In a same-session NVIDIA A100 batch-one benchmark, fusion reduces CUDA p95 decision latency from 10.004 to 5.836 ms relative to the legacy repeated path. We also report negative results: a compact policy is faster but fails a predeclared reliability gate, and a conservative early-exit implementation skips most predictive calls but becomes slower because of synchronization overhead. These findings provide both an algorithmic contribution and a reproducible systems lesson relevant to UAV networking.
