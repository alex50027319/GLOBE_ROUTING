We submit the manuscript entitled **“SwitchGLOBE: Global-to-Local Policy Distillation with Predictive Risk Switching for Decentralized FANET Routing”** for consideration as an Original Research Article in *Ad Hoc Networks*.

Rapid topology changes in flying ad hoc networks create a tension between routing quality and decentralized deployability. Policies trained with global graph information can learn structurally informed next-hop behavior, but the same information is generally unavailable or too expensive during execution. Our manuscript addresses this problem by distilling a privileged global routing teacher into a compact student that uses only one-hop observations. The student is combined with predictive risk switching, which activates a safety-oriented branch only when the nominal local action is vulnerable to a routing hole or imminent link break.

The work fits *Ad Hoc Networks* because it addresses mobile ad hoc routing protocols, mobility-aware and fault-tolerant communication, decentralized protocol design, scalability, and simulation-based performance analysis. The manuscript focuses on the protocol-level question of how training-time global knowledge can be transferred into a deployment-time local policy without online teacher communication.
